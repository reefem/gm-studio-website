Good to know

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for anything involving lash glue, lift solution or tint. It’s free, takes about five minutes, and needs to be done at least 48 hours before your appointment. You can book it on Fresha alongside your treatment.

How long does a Korean lash lift last?

Up to eight weeks, depending on your natural lash cycle. There’s nothing to maintain day to day, so it’s a great option if you want lashes that look done without extensions.

How often should I book infills?

Most clients come back every two to three weeks. If you want a quick top-up sooner, there’s a shorter one-week infill for classic, hybrid and volume sets.

When is the studio open?

Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings, 4pm to 8.30pm. All appointments are booked online through Fresha, and you’ll get an instant confirmation.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available, and the studio is close to public transport links.

Can I buy a gift card?

You can. Gift cards are sold through GM Studio’s Fresha page and can be used towards any treatment.
